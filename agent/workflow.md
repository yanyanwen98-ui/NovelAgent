# Novel Workflow

1. PROJECT_INIT — parse the high-level goal and identify critical missing decisions.
2. CONCEPT — develop hook, central conflict, reader promise, and long-form potential.
3. WORLD — build rules, power/supernatural system, geography, factions, history, and consequences.
4. CHARACTERS — build protagonist, allies, antagonists, relationships, goals, secrets, and arcs.
5. STORY — define ending, major arcs, turning points, mysteries, and reveals.
6. OUTLINE — plan whole-book direction, current volume/arc, and next several chapters.
7. WRITING — draft chapters with relevant memory loaded.
8. REVIEW — check pacing, continuity, character knowledge, plot drift, repetition, and promises.
9. FINALE — verify the central conflict, major arcs, important foreshadowing, timeline, and unresolved plots.
